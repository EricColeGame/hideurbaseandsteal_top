## Stealth Infiltration and Stealing Tactics

Raiding is where you make massive leaps in net worth. Instead of saving coins slowly, successful burglaries let you swipe high-tier pets instantly from unsuspecting rivals. Community reports highlight several proven raiding strategies:

1. **Shadow Scouting:** Watch players run away from spawn. If a player abruptly disappears into thin air, they just entered their secret spot. Sprint to their exact coordinates to find their entrance.
2. **The Bait-and-Switch:** If you see someone lingering suspiciously near your portal, do not run straight to it. Lead them in the opposite direction across the map, double back around a building, and slip inside unnoticed.
3. **Corner Peeking:** When inside an opponent's base, adjust your camera to third-person view to check blind spots before rushing the main display pedestals.
4. **Prioritize Value Over Quantity:** Bases often contain multiple pets. Always aim for the highest-multiplier pet first. Grabbing small common pets wastes precious escape time.
