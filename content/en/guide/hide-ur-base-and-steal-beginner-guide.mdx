## Stealth Infiltration: How to Find and Raid Enemy Bases

Raiding other bases is the quickest way to jumpstart your economy without grinding passive income. Finding an opponent's marker requires sharp observation, patience, and a methodical search pattern.

To discover enemy hideouts, check odd map geometry, dark recesses beneath cliffs, and corners behind building props. Players often leave a trail when sprinting toward their hideout. If you notice a player suddenly vanish into thin air, they just entered their base. Sprint over to their exact coordinate to locate their portal.

Follow this step-by-step checklist during every raid run:

1. **Observe Player Movements:** Stand at an elevated vantage point and watch player paths across the map.
2. **Track the Disappearance:** Identify where an avatar vanished to pinpoint their entrance within a 5-stud radius.
3. **Approach with Stealth:** Avoid running in straight lines; jump or flank to prevent tipping off defensive players.
4. **Breach and Assess:** Step into their marker to teleport into the interior. Quickly identify their highest-tier pet.
5. **Extract Immediately:** Grab the pet and use the exit portal instantly before the owner respawns or detects you.

Community reports emphasize that lingering inside an enemy's base is the number one reason beginners fail their heists. Grab the single most valuable target and leave immediately.
