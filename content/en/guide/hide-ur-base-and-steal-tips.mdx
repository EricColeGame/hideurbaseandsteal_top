### Optimal Placement Locations

| Hiding Spot Type | Risk Level | Visibility Rating | Player Traffic | Recommended Strategy |

| **High Foliage / Bushes** | Medium | Low | Moderate | Excellent early game; drop in outer map trees |

### Advanced Hiding Tactics

1. **Camera Angle Exploitation**: Most Roblox players navigate with a third-person camera angled slightly downward. Placing your marker beneath overhangs, underneath stair ramps, or behind steep geometry forces players to tilt their camera upward to spot it, which rarely happens during casual sprinting.
2. **Color Blending**: Drop your entrance marker on ground textures that closely match the portal's edge lighting. Breaking line-of-sight from popular running routes reduces accidental detections.
3. **Relocation Drills**: Don't leave your base in the same spot for an entire session. If a player sees you disappear into thin air, they now know your general vicinity. Relocate whenever you notice persistent foot traffic nearby.

### Essential Defense Rules

- **Never Walk Straight In While Watched**: If you suspect another player is tailing you, keep running past your real marker. Lead them across the map, double back around an obstacle, and slip into your entrance only when your line-of-sight is completely broken.
- **Secure High-Value Pets First**: When designing your base layout, position your rarest creatures as far from the spawn doorway as the game allows. Every extra second an intruder spends walking inward gives you time to react, respawn, or intercept them.
- **Spend Excess Capital Fast**: Unspent cash cannot be directly stolen like deployed pets, but hoarding currency without turning it into upgrades leaves your infrastructure under-defended. Reinvest your money into pet upgrades and speed boosts regularly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the best hide ur base and steal tips for beginners?
The most crucial tip for beginners is to avoid placing your base marker anywhere near the map spawn point. Always sprint to the outer perimeter, tuck your marker behind environmental props or foliage, and avoid leading other players directly toward your entrance. Reinvest your initial profits into mobility upgrades so you can scout enemy bases much faster.

### Can other players steal all my pets at once?
Infiltrators can generally carry only a limited number of pets per heist. However, if your base location is discovered by multiple players or visited repeatedly while you are away, your entire pen can be drained over several runs. Always rotate your marker location if you suspect your current spot has been compromised.

### What should I do if a player camps my base entrance?
If an opponent camps right outside your portal marker, avoid confronting them without an escape plan. If the game permits, pick up or relocate your base marker to a fresh spot on the map. Alternatively, coordinate an offensive push toward their base while they are busy waiting outside yours.

### How often should I relocate my base marker?
Player reports suggest relocating your base marker every 15 to 20 minutes, or immediately after spotting suspicious players lurking around your area. Regular repositioning keeps persistent scouts guessing and prevents coordinated group raids on your hideout.
