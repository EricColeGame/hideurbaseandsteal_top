## Stealth Strategies: How to Hide Your Base Marker

A common mistake among beginners is placing the bunker hatch near obvious landmarks, spawn areas, or straight walking paths. Experienced raiders sprint across open fields specifically scanning for ground clipping or slightly raised textures.

Community reports suggest several clever positioning tricks that significantly lower the chances of someone discovering your vault:

- **Foliage Camouflage:** Position your portal directly beneath natural foliage, shrubbery, or decorative tree trunks where the ground texture is visually broken.
- **Elevation Traps:** Drop your marker on uneven cliffs or narrow ledges that require awkward platforming to reach. Casual raiders rarely jump along perimeter walls.
