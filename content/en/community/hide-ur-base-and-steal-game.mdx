### Elevation vs. Foliage Hiding

Many novices drop their marker directly inside the nearest bush. While this conceals the glowing ground indicator from immediate horizontal sightlines, seasoned players use high-contrast graphics settings to spot protruding hitboxes. 

Community reports suggest scaling vertical map architecture—such as cliff edges, structural support beams, or roof rafters—offers significantly superior security. Most players looking for bases keep their camera tilted downward toward the ground. Taking the high ground keeps your marker outside standard peripheral vision.

### Protecting Your Investments

When managing your vault, do not place your top-earning companions right beside your underground spawn pad. If an intruder warps into your base, items closest to the arrival point are the first to get snatched.

1. **Stagger Your Layout:** Position low-value common pets near the entrance to act as a physical buffer and time-waster for raiders.
2. **Deposit High-Tier Assets Deeper:** Keep Legendary and Secret pets tucked away in the back corners of your bunker.
3. **Keep Bank Reserves Ready:** Maintain enough liquid currency to re-seed your economy if an unexpected break-in wipes out your current farm.

### Reading Player Movement

If you see an opponent suddenly vanish near a rock formation, do not run straight to that exact pixel immediately. Wait three to five seconds. Inexperienced players often exit their bases repeatedly to check if the coast is clear. 

Once you confirm they are settled inside, approach the coordinates, find their marker, jump through, and snatch their best pets. Remember to immediately clear out of the area upon returning to the surface so you do not lead angry rivals directly to your own secret base.
