## Core Mechanics: Camouflage, Bases, and Raiding

The gameplay loop of this experience diverges significantly from conventional tycoons. Instead of expanding a permanent plot along a predefined street, you carry your base portal with you. 

When you start a session, you are given a deployment marker. Dropping this spot links your character to an instanced underground vault. Inside this vault, you construct pedestals, recruit pet companions, and generate passive cash per second. However, your deployment spot remains visible in the shared open-world hub. If another player locates your marker, they can interact with it, walk directly into your vault, and snatch your most valuable units before teleporting away.

To get started with the engine powering these unique social mechanics, you can read more about game systems on the [official Roblox platform](https://www.roblox.com/).

### The Core Loop Breakdown

1. **Deploy & Conceal:** Place your entrance marker in an obscure part of the map.
2. **Generate Revenue:** Buy pets to harvest passive coins inside your vault.
3. **Scout Rivals:** Roam the map hunting for visible opponent markers.
4. **Execute Raids:** Enter unguarded bases, steal high-tier pets, and extract safely.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens to stolen pets in Hide ur Base and Steal?
When you steal a pet from another player's base, it transfers into your inventory and is removed from the victim's vault. You can immediately place the stolen pet onto your own income pedestals to harvest its cash multiplier, or store it in your safety bank if you want to keep it safe from counter-raids.

### How do I stop players from camping outside my base marker?
If a rival player spots your marker and decides to camp nearby, do not exit the portal immediately. Wait inside your vault or use a secondary exit if your hideout upgrade level supports one. Alternatively, you can pick up your marker from the interface menu and redeploy it in a completely different sector of the map to break the camper's line of sight.

### Can I retrieve a pet that was stolen from my base?
Yes. If you recognize the username of the player who raided your vault, you can track down their deployed marker in the open world and launch a retaliatory raid. If you breach their base before they lock your pet into an armored vault slot, you can reclaim your stolen pet and return it home.

### What is the most reliable way to hide ur base and steal best items consistently?
The most reliable strategy is to deploy your marker high above ground level within tree foliage or cliff seam geometry, equip movement-speed boosting items, and target bases owned by players who are away exploring the map. Always deposit stolen items into protected slots before setting out on your next raid.
