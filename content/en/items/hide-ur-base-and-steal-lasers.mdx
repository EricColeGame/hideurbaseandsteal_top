
## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if an intruder destroys my hide ur base and steal lasers?
Intruders cannot permanently delete your installed structures. If a thief uses an item like a bypass pad, your laser beams will only deactivate for a few seconds before automatically cycling back online. Any permanent upgrades you purchase for your bunker remain tied to your character data.

### Can players jump over all laser barriers in the game?
No. While entry-level Tripwire beams can be cleared with standard jumps, upgraded systems like Crossfire Matrices and Plasma Wall Gates feature vertical hitboxes that cannot be jumped over without specialized tools or speed power-ups. Stacking low and high beams creates a vertical trap wall that prevents simple jumps.

### How do I move my base entrance marker if players discover it?
You can pack up and relocate your entrance marker by interacting with your base management terminal inside your bunker. Relocating your entry point is free and instantly moves your doorway, invalidating the notes of any scouts who discovered your previous hiding spot.

### What is the most cost-effective way to protect rare pets?
The most cost-effective method is creating a bend in your entrance corridor and placing Dual Grid Lasers at the corner. Intruders cannot sprint directly through the turn without brushing the damage hitboxes, which eliminates most players before they can reach your pet habitats.
