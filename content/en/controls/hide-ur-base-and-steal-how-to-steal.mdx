### Phase 1: Locating Secret Base Markers

Every player must place a teleport marker in the world to access their underground bunker. Careless builders often drop these markers near spawn points, tree lines, or behind starter shops.

1. **Check Obstructed Angles:** Inspect the backside of terrain boulders, dark corners beneath ramps, and foliage clusters.
2. **Watch Player Trajectories:** Observe players who sprint away from common areas and suddenly disappear; their vanishing point reveals their portal marker.
3. **Audio Cues:** Entering or exiting an underground room generates a distinct teleport audio chime that sharp ears can trace.

Once you step on an enemy marker, you will immediately teleport inside their private tycoon. The moment you arrive, remain completely silent and crouch or pause behind partition walls. ## Advanced Troubleshooting for Failed Thefts

If you are consistently struggling to complete your robberies, one of the following mechanical issues is likely causing your attempts to fail:

1. **Premature Channel Break:** Releasing your interact button just milliseconds before the circle fills will instantly cancel the steal. Hold the key until your character model visually shoulders the pet.
2. **Ping Spikes:** High server latency can cause a desync between your screen and the server. If your steal input fails to register, try server-hopping to find a region closer to you.
3. **Owner Teleport Lockout:** If the base owner logs out or moves their exterior base marker while you are inside, the bunker will collapse, returning you directly to the central map without loot.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can other players steal my pets while I am inside their base?
Yes. Raids happen simultaneously in real time. If an enemy discovers your marker while you are away raiding another player, they can access your vault and steal your pets unless you have hidden your portal marker effectively.

### What happens if I get eliminated while carrying a stolen pet?
If your character resets or is eliminated by environmental hazards or opposing players before you cross into your own base, the stolen creature drops to the ground and will despawn back to its original owner's pedestal.

### Does hide ur base and steal how to steal work if the owner is actively inside?
Yes, but it is far riskier. The game does not lock interaction prompts when the owner is nearby. However, the owner can physically chase you, contest your path, or force you to drop the pet if they down your character before you reach the portal.

### Why won't the steal prompt appear when I approach a pet?
Community reports suggest this occurs when your avatar is holding an active tool or consumable. Ensure your hands are empty, stand directly against the pet's pedestal, and confirm that the bunker owner has not locked their room using a temporary base protection perk.
