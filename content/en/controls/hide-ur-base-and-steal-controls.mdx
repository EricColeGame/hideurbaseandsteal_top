## Troubleshooting Control Glitches and Performance Issues

Input lag and unresponsive buttons can ruin an otherwise flawless heist. Because this experience processes real-time player states alongside persistent economy saves, system bottlenecks can occasionally delay your commands.

### Common Control Bugs and Fixes

| Issue Encountered | Probable Cause | Quick Fix / Workaround | Prevention Step |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Interact Key (**E**) Unresponsive | Network desync with host server | Step back 3 studs, re-approach object | Wait for the prompt icon to fully highlight white |
| Base Hatch Won't Deploy | Uneven terrain collision | Move to flatter ground geometry | Avoid steep cliff inclines when placing |
| Camera Spinning Wildly | Conflicting Shift Lock settings | Press **Shift** twice or tap **Esc** | Set Camera Mode to "Classic" in settings |
| Mobile Joystick Drifting | Multi-touch screen smudge / oil | Lock virtual pad in Roblox settings | Enable "Dynamic Thumbstick" option |
| Jump Failure on Ledges | Geometry micro-wedges | Diagonal strafe-jump (**W + A/D + Space**) | Approach corners at a 45-degree angle |

Player experience highlights that setting your graphics level to a fixed manual slider (level 3 to 5) rather than "Automatic" dramatically reduces frame drops. Stable frame delivery ensures your **hide ur base and steal controls** register on the very first frame of an interaction prompt.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What are the default hide ur base and steal controls for PC?
The default PC setup uses **W, A, S, D** for character navigation, **Spacebar** to jump, **E** or **Left Click** to interact and steal items, and **F** to place your hidden base marker. You can pan your viewpoint using the **Right Mouse Button** or toggle **Left Shift** for Shift Lock control.

### How do I steal pets from another player's base?
Find an opposing player's hidden entrance marker hidden in the landscape, walk onto it to enter their vault, walk up to their stored pets, and press your interact input (**E** on PC, contextual tap on mobile, or **X / Square** on console). Hold the button until the theft meter fills, then make your way to the exit portal.

### Why won't my base placement button work?
If your deploy command does not register, you are likely attempting to drop the base entrance on invalid geometry, such as vertical walls, player spawns, or overlapping another player's existing marker. Step onto flat, open terrain and try pressing your placement key again.

### Can I customize my hide ur base and steal controls on mobile?
While you cannot entirely re-map individual buttons on mobile, you can customize your experience in the Roblox settings menu. You can switch between "Dynamic Thumbstick" and "Classic Thumbstick," adjust overall camera sensitivity, and move the jump/interaction touch targets to fit your grip.
