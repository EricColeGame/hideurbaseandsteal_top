## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I use the hide ur base and steal teleport marker?
Equip the teleport marker tool from your hotbar, aim at a valid ground surface, and click or tap to place it. Once the pad appears, simply walk your character over it to teleport directly inside your personal base vault.

### Can other players use my teleport marker to get inside my base?
Yes. If another player discovers your hidden teleport marker on the overworld map, they can step on it and instantly warp into your base to steal your pets. Keeping your portal concealed in bushes, cliffs, or water is crucial to protect your stash.

### What should I do if someone finds my base location?
If an enemy discovers your marker, enter your base, secure your pets, and exit back to the overworld. Pick up or recall your teleport pad, relocate to a completely different sector of the map, and deploy your entrance in a fresh hiding spot.

### Does upgrading my base make it harder for intruders to steal?
Tycoon base upgrades expand your plot and unlock higher-earning pet slots, but the teleport portal itself remains accessible to anyone who locates your entrance. Your best defense is always concealment, frequent portal relocation, and constant situational awareness.
