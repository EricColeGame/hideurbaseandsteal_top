## How Base Placement Works in Hide Ur Base and Steal

Before scoping out the best turf, you need to understand how the base portal mechanic operates. When you load into the map, you carry a deployable marker. Dropping this item embeds a custom entry tile onto the terrain surface. Stepping through transports you instantly to your private underground bunker, where your pets generate currency.

However, any roving thief who spots your portal can walk right through it, infiltrate your territory, and snatch your hardest-earned pets. If your entrance sits out in the open or right along the central trade pathways, players will pick your rooms clean in seconds.

### Mechanical Rules for Base Portals

* **Collision & Clipping:** Markers cannot be buried entirely inside solid level geometry, but they can be nestled under low-hanging props or semi-transparent terrain meshes.
* **Proximity Limits:** Players cannot place an entrance directly touching another player’s active marker.
* **Instant Interaction:** Portals do not require an activation timer; any character running over the perimeter triggers entry.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can other players move my portal if they find it?
No. Other players cannot interact with or delete your deployed marker. However, finding your entrance allows them to step straight inside your base to loot unguarded pets, which is why picking secure **hide ur base and steal base locations** remains critical.

### What should I do if a player camps outside my base marker?
If someone is camping your surface entrance, avoid leaving your vault immediately. You can relocate your base by using the in-game reset or shop menu features to recall your marker, or you can switch to a new server to safeguard your current inventory without risking an ambush.

### Do certain graphics settings make base markers easier to find?
Yes. Lowering graphics quality can sometimes remove low-detail ground foliage or shadow shaders, inadvertently exposing markers hidden inside bushes. Conversely, keeping textures sharp makes it easier for you to spot subtle edges of enemy portals hidden against canyon walls.

### How often should I change my base location during a game session?
Based on player experience, moving your portal every 20 to 30 minutes is ideal. High-traffic servers feature active scouts who continuously sweep known hot spots; rotating your marker between perimeter crevices and vertical blind spots ensures your vault stays safe.
