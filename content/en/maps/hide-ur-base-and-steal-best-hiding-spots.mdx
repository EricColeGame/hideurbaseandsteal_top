## The 15-Second Disconnect Window and Safe Exits

A vital mechanic every player must understand is the departure delay: whenever you leave a server or disconnect, your vault remains fully active and raidable for exactly 15 seconds. You cannot avoid a raid simply by abruptly closing the game or returning to the menu while an attacker is standing outside your door.

This mechanic prevents combat-logging and punishes players who panic under pressure. If a hostile player pinpoints your anchor, closing the client leaves your display pads completely undefended. The attacker has a full 15-second window to enter your empty vault, loot your most valuable pets, and escape before the instance closes.

Community reports highlight that aggressive players actively monitor known base spots, waiting for the owner to log off so they can execute an uncontested 15-second cleanout. If you find yourself in an unwinnable server with predatory players camping your door, the safest strategy is to deploy defensive traps, bait the raider into a respawn loop, quickly relocate your anchor to an obscure corner of the map, and then exit safely.
