## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if I get caught during a heist?
If the base owner returns and tags you while you are attempting to take a pet, the channel breaks and you are typically expelled back to the surface spawn. Any pets you were channeling remain with the original owner.

### Can other players steal my pets while I am offline?
Player experience shows that your base remains active only while you are connected to the server session. When you disconnect, your entrance marker disappears from the map, keeping your pets safe from offline raids until you rejoin.

### How does hide ur base and steal stealing compare to normal tycoon progression?
While purchasing pets through the shop provides guaranteed passive growth, engaging in active raiding accelerates your progression by several hours. Stealing allows you to skip early cash grinding by acquiring pets that higher-tier players left under-defended.

### Can I relocate my entrance marker after placing it?
Yes. You can reposition your entrance marker whenever you feel your current hiding spot has been compromised by server rivals. Always relocate immediately if you notice repeat intruders wandering into your territory.
