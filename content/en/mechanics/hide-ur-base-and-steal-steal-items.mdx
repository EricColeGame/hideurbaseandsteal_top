If an opponent discovers your entrance marker, they can step directly onto it to enter your interior base. Once inside, they can interact with your pets to steal them. To prevent this, place your marker in remote, visually obscured locations.

### How do I hide ur base and steal steal items effectively as a solo player?
Solo players should focus on vertical hiding spots that require precise parkour jumps to access. Once your entrance is secured high above ground level, run stealth routes across lower valleys to look for poorly concealed enemy portals.

### Can players steal items back after I loot them?
Yes. Stolen pets reside inside your base pen once brought home. If the victim (or another competitor) tracks down your new base location, they can enter and take those pets back. Regularly relocating your base entrance prevents revenge raids.

### What is the best strategy if my base gets raided constantly?
