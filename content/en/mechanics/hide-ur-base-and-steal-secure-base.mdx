### Essential Rules for Marker Placement

*   **Avoid Flat Horizons:** Never deploy your marker on uniform textures where its color outline sharply contrasts with the grass or floor.
*   **Leverage Environmental Clutter:** Place your marker adjacent to natural rock piles, underneath fallen logs, or deep inside decorative geometry.
*   **Watch the Particle Effects:** Keep in mind that some items and player trails produce lighting cues that can trace straight back to your portal pad.
*   **Rotate Locations Regularly:** If a rival player spots you entering your base, relocate your marker immediately to another quadrant of the map.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the most important step to hide ur base and steal secure base assets?
The most critical factor is portal placement. Never place your entrance marker in open, brightly lit terrain or near standard travel paths. Embed it inside visual clutter, environmental shadows, or irregular ground terrain to prevent scouts from stumbling upon it.

### Can other players steal my pets if I am inside my base?
Yes. If another player locates your overworld marker and steps onto it, they will teleport directly inside your vault regardless of whether you are present. Inside, they can pick up unprotected pets and flee through the exit unless you actively obstruct or counter them.

### What should I do if an opponent discovers my hidden marker?
Move your base marker immediately. Once a player notes your location, they will return periodically to check for newly purchased pets. Evacuate your high-tier assets into your inventory or relocate the portal to a completely different quadrant of the map.

### How do I recover quickly after being raided?
Keep a reserve stash of starter or mid-tier pets in your secondary storage rather than leaving everything active on your vault floor. You can also target less guarded beginner bases across the map to rapidly replenish lost assets through counter-raiding.
